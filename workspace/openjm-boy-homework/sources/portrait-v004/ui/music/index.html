Of course! The water cycle is water’s journey around Earth. Here’s a simple explanation for your presentation:

Evaporation: The sun warms water in rivers, lakes and oceans, turning it into invisible water vapour.

Condensation: As the vapour cools, it forms tiny droplets that gather into clouds.

Precipitation: Water falls from clouds as rain or snow.

Collection: Water gathers in rivers, lakes and oceans, while some soaks into the ground. Then the journey begins again!

You can remember it as: up, clouds, down, and around!

Source: USGS Water Science School
