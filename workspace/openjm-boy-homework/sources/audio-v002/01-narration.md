Output: nine separate dry voice files, basenames `openjm-N01-v002` through `openjm-N09-v002`. These are proposed outputs, currently missing.

## Delivery direction

Use this to choose/direct the voice; **do not paste this paragraph into the TTS spoken-text field**. If designing a voice, use the description in Voice Design, save one selected voice, then reuse it for every phrase. An existing matching library voice is also suitable.

> Conversational adult woman speaking clear English, with soft warmth, gentle enthusiasm and a natural smile. She sounds curious and encouraging, as though helping someone explore an idea. Moderate, unhurried pace; clear consonants and relaxed breaths. Keep the energy intimate but fully voiced, never whispered or breathy. No exaggerated advertising cadence, dramatic emphasis, baby talk, character voice or sung words. Say the brand “Open Jay Em,” with Jay and Em clearly separated. Record clean, dry speech only: no music, reverb or effects.

Choose one matching voice and one TTS model for the whole set. Multilingual v2 is a suitable consistency-oriented starting choice if available. Keep the accent, voice ID and settings fixed between takes. Natural English punctuation is sufficient; the spoken blocks below need no tags or timing markup.

Generate **one block at a time**. Download each original immediately under its matching basename. Aim to finish naturally within its window; silence need not fill the remaining window. Allow a short clean tail after the last word. If a phrase overruns, regenerate with slightly lighter pauses or revise its wording before mixing; do not accelerate the whole soundtrack or alter the picture.
