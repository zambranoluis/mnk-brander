# Commercial preparation workflow

For new video work, resolve Filmmaker (`mnk-filmmaker`, alias `filmmaker`) and use its source STUDIO/PRODUCTION owners. This local workflow remains available for existing Brander films under their recorded owner; preserve their locations, histories and editable associations.

Use [PRODUCTION](../../PRODUCTION.md) for studio work and retention, [COMMERCIAL](../../COMMERCIAL.md) for film requirements, and [creative development](CREATIVE-DEVELOPMENT.md) only when expressive decisions remain open. Enter at the requested step and stop at the requested outcome. A storyboard or prompt request does not imply rendering.

## Five preparation steps

1. Brief: establish audience, message, intended response, duration/formats, constraints and acceptance. Separate verified product facts from proposed staging.
2. Script: specify what happens, spoken/on-screen copy, sound, timing and intended transitions. Preserve named behavior and approved direction during revisions.
3. Storyboard: define stable shot IDs, action, framing/camera, start/end states, duration, sound and references. Scene grouping retains shot identity.
4. References and assets: select and inspect master references and variants together; use the commercial owner's [continuity and generation requirements](../../COMMERCIAL.md#reference-continuity-and-generation). Record fixed appearance and permitted state changes.
5. Handoff: supply only requested material with explicit dependencies, missing inputs, instructions and actual readiness. Follow the commercial owner's [handoff checks](../../COMMERCIAL.md#handoff).

## Self-contained generation prompts

For external execution, provide purpose/model/mode, output format, subject/action, setting, camera/composition, timing where relevant, sound/text, fixed details, allowed changes and exclusions needed for the shot. Assign exact available files and reference roles through the model's documented mechanism. Include settings, dependencies and inspection criteria. Order reference-building prompts before dependent shots; label proposed filenames as missing outputs. Repeated prose does not establish visual continuity or successful generation.

## Revisions and delivery

The same task continues through revisions. [Commercial reuse](../../COMMERCIAL.md#reuse-and-preservation) keeps unchanged inputs and tools shared within a production, isolates explicit target formats and rebuilds affected stages only. [Production package requirements](../../COMMERCIAL.md#production-package) apply when an independent handoff or separate production is requested. A preview, completed edit or technical pass does not request packaging or archiving.
